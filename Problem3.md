### Problem 3 - Largest Subrange (20 points)

+ Given an integer array $A$ of size $n$, find the index pair $i$ and $j$ that maximizes $S = \sum_{k=i}^{j}A[k]$, return $S$ as the answer.

### Report - Largest Subrange
**Brute-force.** Try every pair i, j and keep a running sum. $T(n)=\Theta(n^2)$.

**Divide-and-conquer.** Split in the middle. Best subarray is either in the left half, in the right half, or crosses the middle. The crossing one is best suffix of left plus best prefix of right, found in $O(n)$. $T(n)=2T(n/2)+\Theta(n)$, by Master method $T(n)=\Theta(n\log{n})$.

**Time (n = 10^5, ns):** brute ~4079419130, smart ~3088270.
